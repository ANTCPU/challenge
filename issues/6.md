## Gate d13 — Peer Review · +48%

Review one other challenger's Week 2 PR.

---

## Your Task

Find an open PR in this repo from another challenger.
Leave a review comment that includes:

1. **What works** — one specific thing, not just "great job"
2. **What to improve** — one specific suggestion with reasoning
3. **A question** — something you genuinely want to know
   about their approach

### The standard

> "Great work" is not a review.  
> Tell them what to change and why.  
> Specific > Encouraging.

A good review takes 10 minutes and makes their build better.
That's the job.

## How to Complete This Gate

2. Leave your review comment on their PR
3. **Reply to this issue** with a link to the PR you reviewed
